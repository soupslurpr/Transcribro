Recognition sources

Transcribro Medium recognition runs in the pure-Rust engine in engine/.
Original model values and tokenizer:
https://download.moonshine.ai/model/medium-streaming-en/quantized_26_08_21
The exact eight source-file hashes are in docs/moonshine-model.json; the
packaged model.json pins the reversible archive and its compressed
representation. The shipped archive is the uncompressed medium.trm; the
app memory-maps it in place and extracts nothing.
Model, stream/tokenizer policy and embedded inner Silero source:
https://github.com/moonshine-ai/moonshine/tree/234f60faa0eb388b01cdf7e60aca232af37aefda
See Moonshine-LICENSE.txt and Moonshine-MODIFICATIONS.txt.

Outer Silero VAD 6.2: a pure-Rust port (engine/src/outer_vad.rs) of
whisper.cpp's ggml Silero network:
https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/tree/927cfce34f31707e17f2bff35c349632fb9e2c3a
The model container it reads:
https://huggingface.co/ggml-org/whisper-vad/blob/9ffd54a1e1ee413ddf265af9913beaf518d1639b/ggml-silero-v6.2.0.bin
See NOTICE.txt for the original Silero and ggml notices.

Zstandard 1.5.7 (BSD-3-Clause) is a build-time tool only; no Zstandard
code is included in the app:
https://github.com/facebook/zstd/tree/v1.5.7

All build acquisitions and conversion inputs are pinned in
speech/native-inputs.lock.json. Native libraries and DEX are stored
uncompressed in the APK.
