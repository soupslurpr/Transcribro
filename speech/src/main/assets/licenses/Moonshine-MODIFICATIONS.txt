Transcribro uses original English Medium Streaming weights from Moonshine
234f60faa0eb388b01cdf7e60aca232af37aefda, under the MIT license.

The native fixed-model runtime implements the frontend, encoder, decoder,
inner Silero VAD, segmentation and binary tokenization. Streaming policy is
adapted from core/transcriber.cpp, core/voice-activity-detector.cpp,
core/moonshine-streaming-model.cpp and core/bin-tokenizer/bin-tokenizer.cpp.
It retains bounded look-behind, full final encoding, speculative decoding,
20 final / 6.5 preview tokens per second, and skipped unfinished analysis
under queue pressure. The JNI bridge retains the outer Silero detector,
preview delivery, cancellation, manual boundaries and the final silent drain.

The archive reversibly permutes integer matrices and the tied embedding
without changing original weights, scales, zero points, biases or FP32 values.
Zstandard compresses the archive losslessly. The pinned source's embedded
16 kHz Silero values and original tokenizer are preserved. ARM64 executes a
packed head; x86_64 expands the same original head values for MLAS execution.
Baseline x86 projections also reconstruct original unsigned integer codes
for the pinned MLAS dispatcher; their affine arithmetic is unchanged.
No Moonshine runtime library or ONNX graph/session runtime is packaged.
The app does include Microsoft ONNX Runtime MLAS numerical-kernel source.
